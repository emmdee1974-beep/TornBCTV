The Punchbags Files

What started as an Elimination team election became a running TornBC TV satire: Malice, Pinguux, campaign propaganda, team drama, mock breaking-news coverage and the aftermath. The clips below are the surviving video record.

This page is a retrospective of fan-made satire and event coverage, not an accusation or official Torn record.

How it unfolded

01 • Election
Team Punchbags picks leadership. The Malice campaign begins and TornBC TV discovers politics.
02 • Propaganda
Campaign posters, attack ads, mock endorsements and increasingly ridiculous state-media aesthetics.
03 • Drama
Discord conflict and leadership friction become breaking-news material.
04 • Fallout
Removal, reaction, protests and the event itself turn into a serialized comedy/news chronicle.
05 • Archive
The originals may be scattered, but the surviving YouTube uploads preserve the story.
