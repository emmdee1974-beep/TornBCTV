LoneBlackBear wins 110% — somehow

TORN CITY — TornBC TV can exclusively report that LoneBlackBear, Captain of Nine Lives, has won an astonishing 110% of the vote. The result has been declared completely unquestionable despite the small technical issue that the competition has not actually been decided yet.

Officials cite overwhelming support from voters, future voters and voters who apparently have not yet been informed that they voted. When questioned about how a candidate can receive more than the total available vote, authorities responded simply: “That is the people’s will.”

“We haven’t won yet,” sources close to the campaign admitted. “But we’re going to.”
