Rain falls over Torn City. Neon flickers against wet brick and the streets shine black beneath the lamps. Somewhere in an alley, two rival factions have decided words are no longer enough.

The fight is already underway when LoneBlackBear steps out of the rain.

He growls. One clawed hand drags across the brick wall beside him, cutting deep grooves into it. In the other is a long black combat knife. The alley goes silent. Nobody needs to ask who just arrived.

Above them, another figure is already in position.

Lexi2Legit lies on a rooftop overlooking the street, night vision trained on the scene, a sniper rifle settled against her shoulder. The sharp clack-clack of the bolt carries through the rain.

Nobody moves.

The camera pulls back from the alley, rises above the rooftops and into a lightning-filled Torn City skyline.
